# JSLC and Conquer

The supply line is fragile. The orbital laser is not subtle. The penguins have declared all fishies strategic assets.

Command a force in a satirical war over territory, resources, and legitimacy.
JSLC fields combined arms, layered defenses, aviation, and a navy.
AoI fights through cheap mobile forces, concealment, and tunnels.
The Directorate of the Penguins brings ancient technology and Antarctic ambition.

Build your base. Scout the approaches. Secure the next supply source.
Protect your support units while you push toward the objective.
Ships carry the landing force; aircraft need a way home.
Ground units sink in deep water.

Target choice matters.
The intended political rules make protected actors consequential, even beneath an orbital weapon's footprint.
Those systems and the full campaign remain incomplete in the current game.

The campaign begins beneath Diego Garcia and follows the fleet toward contested shores and Kharg.
The later branch turns inland through southern Iran or along the southern sea route.
Most authored missions remain previews; Shorehead has a playable adaptation.

Open **Factions** for units and technology.
