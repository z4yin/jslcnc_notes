# JSLC & Conquer: Campaign Instructions

## Act I: The Undersea Vanguard
- **Map 1: Diego Garcia Abyss** - Tutorial. Teaches deep-sea base building and sub-surface logistics.
- **Map 2: Operation Shorehead** - Execute a contested amphibious beach landing.
- **Map 3: Amphibious Thrust** - Secure the beachhead and hold off initial counter-attacks.

## Act II: The Gulf Escalation
- **Map 4: Blue Aurora Elevator** - Complex 4-phase state machine objective. You must clear the mast and manage ship lock/unlock overrides.
- **Map 5: Kharg Island** - Cut off enemy resource flows while managing diplomatic lawfare events from the off-map Francesca Albanese director.

## Act III: The Divergence
After Kharg Island, the campaign branches based on player choice:
- **Path A: Southern Iran** - A brutal, conventional armored push into the mainland.
- **Path B: South Yemen** - Coastal interdiction relying heavily on naval supremacy and the newly secured amphibious routes.
