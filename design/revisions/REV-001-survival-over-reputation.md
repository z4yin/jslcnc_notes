---

## 1. Doctrinal Foundation: "Rather Be Alive Than Loved"

In the real world, Israeli / JSOC military doctrine does **not** allow international diplomatic pressure or foreign opinion polls to paralyze self-defense. When an active existential threat is firing on frontline forces or base infrastructure, the weapons **must** fire. Artificial "Rules of Engagement Locks" that disable a player's own artillery or airstrikes while under fire feel gamey, patronizing, and fundamentally contrary to authentic doctrine.

---

## 2. The Real World Impacts of Reputational Harm

When Public Legitimacy drops and international pressure mounts, the battlefield consequences are **logistical, economic, and civil**, not kinetic:

### A. Supply Chain & Munitions Embargoes (Factory Delays)
- Foreign governments and international suppliers delay export clearances for munitions, dual-use electronics, and vehicle spare parts.
- **In-Game Mechanic:**
  - When Legitimacy drops below 50%, factory and airfield build times increase by **+25% to +60%** (reinforcements arrive slower).
  - Heavy weapons delivery (e.g. 2,000-lb JDAM restocks for F-15s/F-35s) incurs longer rearm intervals.
  - Credit funding streams from international patrons are throttled.

### B. Protester Mobs & Gate Blockades (Civil Disruption)
- Unarmed civil protest movements assemble outside JSOC base perimeters, naval harbor entrances, and supply highway chokepoints.
- **In-Game Mechanic:**
  - Demonstrator mobs physically block perimeter gates and access roads.
  - Logistics trucks (ammo carriers, fuel tankers) are halted at the gate until the protest is dispersed.
  - The commander must employ **non-lethal crowd control** (Military Police water cannons, acoustic LRAD arrays) to clear the route, or risk severe PR collapse if lethal force is used.

### C. Maritime Port & Canal Strikes
- International labor unions and civil flotillas stage sit-ins across canal mouths and harbor channels, obstructing combat dredging and deep-draft naval egress.

---

## 3. Kinetic Freedom Guaranteed

Under this revised model:
- **Weapons Never Lock:** The commander can *always* order artillery strikes, airstrikes, and defensive bombardments. Survival is prioritized above all.
- **Consequential Friction:** Disregarding public legitimacy doesn't freeze your guns—it strangles your supply lines, slows down your factory output, and swarms your gates with civil unrest.
